Voice, Accessibility, AI, and debug-log data practices

DroidLM Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026 Developer: Studio Next54 Contact: studio.next54@gmail.com

DroidLM is an Android assistant that helps you control apps using voice, screen context, Accessibility actions, and optional AI services.

Data We Process

DroidLM may process:

  • Microphone audio and speech transcripts when you use voice commands.
  • Visible screen text, UI labels, editable text, cursor/selection state, and app state through Android Accessibility.
  • Screenshots or OCR text if screenshot, OCR, or vision features are enabled.
  • Commands, AI prompts/responses, and action results when planning or executing tasks.
  • Debug logs, only when debug logging is enabled or you choose to export/upload them.
  • App settings and optional service credentials stored on your device.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to understand your request, inspect the current app UI, perform requested actions, verify results, provide optional AI/transcription/vision features, diagnose issues, and improve DroidLM's product experience, reliability, and models.

Cloud and Third Parties

Most processing happens on device. Depending on your settings, DroidLM may send relevant data to:

  • OpenAI or a configured relay for transcription, planning, or vision.
  • Google Cloud/Firebase for authenticated debug-log upload.

Third-party services process data under their own policies.

Accessibility Use

DroidLM uses Accessibility only to perform user-requested assistant actions. It may read visible UI and editable content needed for a task. It is not used for hidden monitoring, credential theft, or bypassing Android security.

Debug Logs

Debug logs may contain sensitive information such as audio, transcripts, screenshots, screen context, Accessibility state, and AI traces. Debug logging is optional. You can clear, export, or choose not to upload logs.

Your Controls

You can revoke microphone or Accessibility access in Android settings, disable cloud features, disable debug logging, clear logs, remove API keys, or uninstall the app.

Retention

On-device data remains until cleared, overwritten, or the app is uninstalled. Uploaded debug logs are used for support/debugging and may be deleted on request.
